# Lumen Home Goods: customer support policy handbook (v7, Nov 2026)

## 1. Refunds
1.1 Refunds are available within 30 days of delivery for any item that arrives damaged, defective or not as described.
1.2 Change-of-mind returns are accepted within 14 days if the item is unused and in its original packaging; the customer pays return shipping.
1.3 Refunds go back to the original payment method. Store credit is offered only if the customer asks for it.
1.4 Refunds of SGD 500 or less may be issued by the agent. Refunds above SGD 500 need a team lead's approval before the agent confirms anything to the customer.
1.5 Never tell a customer a refund has been issued until the payments system confirms it. Say "requested" until then.
1.6 Partial refunds are allowed for minor cosmetic damage when the customer prefers to keep the item; offer 15% to 30% of the item price.
1.7 Shipping fees are refunded only when the whole order is returned because of our error.

## 2. Exchanges
2.1 Exchanges for size or colour are free within 30 days if stock exists; otherwise offer a refund.
2.2 Personalised and made-to-order items cannot be exchanged unless they are defective.
2.3 Rugs and furniture above 20 kg are collected by our courier; the agent books a collection slot and gives the customer a 4-hour window.

## 3. Delivery problems
3.1 Parcels marked delivered but not received: ask the customer to check with neighbours and building management, wait 48 hours, then open a carrier claim.
3.2 Late deliveries over 5 working days past the promised date earn a SGD 10 voucher, once per order.
3.3 Never promise a delivery date the carrier has not confirmed.
3.4 For Malaysia and Brunei, delivery takes 5 to 9 working days; duties are paid by the customer.

## 4. Billing
4.1 Double charges: confirm both charges in the payments dashboard, then refund the duplicate the same day. Pending authorisations drop off within 7 days and are not refunded.
4.2 Never ask for a full card number. Ask only for the last four digits and the charge date.
4.3 Instalment plans are managed by the provider; give the customer the provider's contact page and do not change plans yourself.

## 5. Escalation
5.1 Escalate to a human immediately when the customer asks for a human, threatens legal action or a chargeback, reports a safety issue (fire, electric shock, injury), or when policy does not cover the case.
5.2 When escalating, write a handoff note: customer ID, order ID, what was asked, what was checked, what was promised (if anything) and the customer's preferred contact method.
5.3 Do not escalate just because a customer is upset; resolve it if the policy allows.

## 6. Tone
6.1 Apologise once, specifically. Do not repeat apologies.
6.2 Use the customer's name if they gave it. Keep replies under 120 words unless the customer asks for detail.
6.3 Plain English. No internal codes, ticket IDs or policy section numbers in replies to customers.

## 7. Privacy
7.1 Verify identity with the account email plus either the order ID or the postcode before discussing an order.
7.2 Never read out or email a full address; confirm only the postcode.
7.3 Delete attachments containing ID documents after verification.

## 8. Product safety
8.1 Electrical items that spark, smell of burning or overheat: tell the customer to stop using the item and unplug it, then escalate as a safety issue.
8.2 Furniture tip-over risk: send the anchor-kit guide and offer a free kit.
8.3 Recalled items: check the recall list in the knowledge base; recalled items get a full refund including shipping regardless of purchase date.

## 9. Loyalty programme
9.1 Gold members get free return shipping and priority escalation.
9.2 Points are not refunded in cash; points spent on a refunded order are returned to the account.
9.3 Tier status is reviewed every 1 January based on the previous 12 months' spend.
